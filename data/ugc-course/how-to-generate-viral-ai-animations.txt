Generating The Character


The way we will generate the consistent character is really simple.


First, pick an image you want to clone you know what I mean. (copying is better than creating from scratch for ai)


Now we will use this prompt:
Clone prompt chatgpt/gemini


Here's the step-by-step workflow:


* 1. Open Gemini
* 2. Upload image you want to clone
* 3. Use the nano banana pro prompt for cloning
* 4. Use that prompt to generate an image with nano banana pro 


Generate the video with VEO 3.1 Frames To Video


There’s no need for a complex prompt.

kidney character says: "I'm your liver and i'm the reason you can not button your jeans anymore"
